DRAWER · SLIDING PANEL PRIMITIVE
Anatomy
Panel
ink-3 bg · slides over stage · z-index 3
Slide
translateY(−100%→0) · ease-swing · dur-panel 360ms
Anchor
top-edge or bottom-edge · full viewport width
Handle
28px bar · torn SVG edge · 2× 36×3px ivory-4 grip
Tear
SVG path · cut-paper zig-zag · ink-3 fill · butt caps
Scrim
opacity 0→1 · ease-brush · var(--scrim) overlay
States
closed (off-stage) · peek · partial · open
Dismiss
tap handle · click scrim · ESC key

Drawers slide in/out via transform: translateY() — never opacity. Top drawers anchor at the top edge (translateY(-100%→0)); bottom drawers anchor at the foot (translateY(100%→0)). One torn-paper handle per drawer; one brass signal max per stage. All 8 variants are independently togglable — click the handle or scrim.
