04 — Spacing & Shape

Cut, don't carve.

Edges are crisp. Radii are tiny. The poster aesthetic doesn't bevel, doesn't blur, doesn't round.

Marks are flat poster shapes — never gradients, never strokes-on-fills, never with drop shadows. They appear at most one-per-section, large (48–96px), as a typographic anchor. Inline they shrink to 8–12px and stay neutral.
