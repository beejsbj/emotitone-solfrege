The Thesis

Two systems. One stage. The music is the colour.

Today the app is a mish-mash because it tries to do everything everywhere at once: jazz display type fights neon notes fights glassy panels fights terminal code. The fix isn't to pick one — it's to give each its job.

01
Stage / Paper

A dark room.
Cream cut-outs.

The chrome lives on a near-black stage. Cream paper panels stand in front of it like pinned poster cuttings — never both colours fighting on the same surface.

02
Two colour systems

UI is warm-neutral.
Music is chromatic.

The interface palette is six warm greys plus four poster accents. The chromatic 12-tone wheel is reserved for note tiles, sequencer pulses, and visual effects — never buttons, never borders.

03
Two voices

Jazz speaks.
Mono whispers.

Lets Jazz for everything the user reads and remembers. JetBrains Mono for everything the system says back: timestamps, code, metadata, deltas.

The rule

Chrome is warm-neutral.
Music is chromatic.

If you're tempted to colour a button blue because that note is blue, stop. The button is chrome. It's holding the note, not being it. Buttons stay paper, panels stay panel.

06 — Motion · the room breathes

If it doesn't move with the music, it's furniture.

EmotiTone is a sketchpad you play with your fingertips. The whole room — panels, tabs, knobs, the tiles themselves — should feel alive on the beat. Stillness is the silent state.

Persona-5 tabs · the offcut slide

The active chip is a torn paper rectangle, skewed −8°, with a 2px ink shadow. It slides between positions over a black-banner streak, briefly stretching like a smear before snapping flat. Click each label.

Anatomy
  • Active layer: paper fill, skew(-8deg), translate to position
  • Shadow layer: ink, +2px / +2px, follows with 40ms lag
  • Streak: black bar widens during transit, shrinks on settle
  • Ease: Swing, 320ms · text re-skews to upright
When to use

Top-level mode switchers (Anim/Freq/Color/Popup/Scope, Sound/All Sounds). Avoid for low-stakes filters — the smear is loud. For those, fall back to the quiet segmented in §5.

09 — Top drawer · the curtain

No modals. The ceiling drops.

Visual presets, instrument picker, settings — anything that today opens as a centred dialog — instead pulls down from the top edge like a stage curtain. The app stays visible underneath; the drawer commits to the page itself, never floats over it.

Drawer · live demo

07 — Don't

Five things to stop doing.

01 — Music colour as chrome

Buttons, dividers, and tab indicators in note-blue or note-pink. The whole system loses its primary signal — chromatic now means nothing.

— No.
02 — Stacking Jazz on Jazz

Two display sizes side-by-side compete for the same eye. One must drop to Mono caps.

Sound All sounds 992
03 — Glassmorphism

Backdrop blurs and translucent panels. The poster aesthetic is opaque, ink, paper.

A glassy panel

Looks like every SaaS dashboard. Drop it.

04 — Gradient backgrounds

Fade-from-purple-to-blue is not jazz, it's Vue 2014. The stage glow is the only gradient on screen.

05 — Decorative emoji & SVG slop

🎹 ⭐ ✨ in labels and headers. The cut-paper marks already do this job; emoji read as filler.
