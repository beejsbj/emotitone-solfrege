07 — Music marks · bits & boops

Notation, cut from the same paper.

Functional cut-paper marks (§4) handle structure. These angular notation glyphs handle music: dropped into headers, captions, status, empty states. Two weights — paper (cream) for chrome, chromatic when riding a played note.
