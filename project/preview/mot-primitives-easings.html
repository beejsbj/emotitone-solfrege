Five curves do all the work. Each carries a verb: Swing overshoots, Stab snaps in, Brush fades, Sustain tracks a constant rate, Bend tears and recovers. The named uses are not exhaustive — but if your gesture doesn't fit one of these names, the gesture is probably wrong.
