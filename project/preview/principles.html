PRINCIPLES

The fix isn't to pick one mood — it's to give each its job.

01
Rule
Don't mix chrome & music on one surface
UI never borrows from Music. Music never borrows from UI.
01 · Don't
Music colour as chrome

Painting buttons in note-blue or dividers in note-pink. The whole system loses its primary signal — chromatic now means nothing.

02
Rule
Don't paint a button by its note
The button is chrome. It's holding the note, not being it.
02 · Don't
Music colour as chrome

Painting buttons in note-blue or dividers in note-pink. The whole system loses its primary signal — chromatic now means nothing.

03
Rule
Don't double up on Jazz sizes
Two Jazz sizes adjacent = noisy. Drop labels to Mono caps.
02 · Don't
Stack Jazz on Jazz

Two display sizes side by side compete for the same eye. One must drop to Mono caps; otherwise no anchor wins.

04
Rule
Don't bevel, blur or round the poster
Edges crisp. Radii tiny. No drop shadows on poster shapes.
03 · Don't
Glassmorphism

Backdrop blurs and translucent panels make every SaaS dashboard. The poster aesthetic is opaque — ink, paper, no blur.

A glassy panel

Looks like every SaaS dashboard. Drop it.

05
Rule · no paired antipattern
Don't hold still
If it doesn't move with the music, it's furniture. Stillness = silent state.
04 · Don't · no paired rule
Decorative emoji & SVG slop

Emoji and clip-art glyphs in labels and headers. The cut-paper marks and music glyphs already do this job — emoji read as filler.
