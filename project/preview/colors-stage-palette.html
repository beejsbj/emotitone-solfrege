BRASS · #E0A93A
USE RARELY
The lone accent — applied as a finish, not a fill. Borrowed from sax keys, trumpet bells, cymbal edges. Whenever it appears it carries the gradient + slow sheen sweep — never as a flat colour.
Reserve for one signal at a time: active record, the captured value, a single beat lit on the metronome, the brand wordmark on a hero.
Never as a background fill on chrome. Never on borders. Never on text smaller than 12px.
APPLIED: REC 120 CAPTURED VALUE LIT KEY Emotitone
Decorative bits — posters, stickers, illustrations, the chromatic note ring — are free to carry full colour. Chrome is not. Add the .brass class (or .brass-text for type) to inherit fill + sheen automatically.
