# EmotiTone patterns and Looper

Phrases hold musical material. The Looper describes which phrases are playing together.

## Language

**Phrase**: A short piece of music with its recorded context and its own length. Take, Recent, Kept and Library describe where a phrase lives.

**Looper**: The current set of playing phrases sharing a tempo and bar grid.

**Membership**: A phrase's place and playback choices within a Looper or saved Loop. It refers to the phrase without owning another copy of its music.

**Loop**: A named, saved set of memberships that can be opened together.

**Bar origin**: The position of bar one on the shared musical clock, established by the first phrase to play.

**Pin**: A membership choice that keeps the phrase's recorded key and mode as the live musical context changes.

**Latch**: The choice to let completed takes join the playing set automatically.
