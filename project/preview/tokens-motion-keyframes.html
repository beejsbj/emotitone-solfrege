Rhythm · Beat
beat
Global quarter-note scale pulse. Applied via .beat-pulse
pulse
Knob ring breathes on every quarter note
strobe
Panel wire heats on the downbeat (bar 1), steps(1)
bob
Tile drops 2 px on the &-of-beat, staggered per tile
Transition
cut-slide-in
Off-cut tab swap — skewX + translateX + fade. Ease-stab
rip
Paper corner tears off top-right of tile on fire
Lydian Locrian Dorian Aeolian
rip-mode
Cross-fade-rip: old tears up, new slides in. Ease-bend
lift
Tile rises 3 px on tap, returns by 34 %. Ease-stab
smear
Horizontal stretch-snap between two positions
Feedback · Flash · Glow
ring
Chromatic ring expands to 1.5× and fades on tile fire
ring-flash
Brass ring strobes on the downbeat (knob outer edge)
flash
Standalone opacity hit for feedback (no displacement)
glow
Ambient note hue glow — box-shadow pulse on active key
paper-rip-flash
Composed: lift + rip + ring in concert. No single keyframe

Twelve named keyframes in the system. Five are defined globally in colors_and_type.css (beat, cut-slide-in, brass-sheen); seven live in motion-primitive cards. Four are pending definition: smear, flash, glow, paper-rip-flash. The five "gesture eases" (swing / stab / brush / sustain / bend) are easing curves, not keyframes — shown here on the shared ease-run track for comparison.
