06 — Motion · the room breathes

If it doesn't move with the music, it's furniture.

EmotiTone is a sketchpad you play with your fingertips. The whole room — panels, tabs, knobs, the tiles themselves — should feel alive on the beat. Stillness is the silent state.

The room beats · HiFi-Rush rule

Drop the dedicated "beat shapes." Instead the chrome itself moves: panels micro-scale, wire borders flash mustard on the downbeat, knob rings pulse, tiles bob 2-3px on the & of every beat. Click the play head to feel it.

Persona-5 tabs · the offcut slide

The active chip is a torn paper rectangle, skewed −8°, with a 2px ink shadow. It slides between positions over a black-banner streak, briefly stretching like a smear before snapping flat. Click each label.

Anatomy
  • Active layer: paper fill, skew(-8deg), translate to position
  • Shadow layer: ink, +2px / +2px, follows with 40ms lag
  • Streak: black bar widens during transit, shrinks on settle
  • Ease: Swing, 320ms · text re-skews to upright
When to use

Top-level mode switchers (Anim/Freq/Color/Popup/Scope, Sound/All Sounds). Avoid for low-stakes filters — the smear is loud. For those, fall back to the quiet segmented in §5.

Note-played · paper-rip flash

When a tile fires, it lifts 2px, paper-rips at one corner, and emits a single chromatic ring matching its hue. No particle burst, no sparkle.
