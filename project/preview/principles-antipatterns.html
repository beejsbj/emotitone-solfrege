PRINCIPLES · FIVE THINGS TO STOP DOING
01 · Don't
Music colour as chrome

Painting buttons in note-blue or dividers in note-pink. The whole system loses its primary signal — chromatic now means nothing.

02 · Don't
Stack Jazz on Jazz

Two display sizes side by side compete for the same eye. One must drop to Mono caps; otherwise no anchor wins.

Sound All sounds 992
03 · Don't
Glassmorphism

Backdrop blurs and translucent panels make every SaaS dashboard. The poster aesthetic is opaque — ink, paper, no blur.

A glassy panel

Looks like every SaaS dashboard. Drop it.

04 · Don't
Gradient backgrounds

Fade-from-purple-to-blue isn't jazz; it's Vue 2014. The stage glow is the only gradient on screen; brass is the only metallic.

05 · Don't
Decorative emoji & SVG slop

🎹 ⭐ ✨ in labels and headers. The cut-paper marks and music glyphs already do this job — emoji read as filler.

🎹Piano ⭐Favourite ✨New preset!
Every "don't" is shown in-situ — broken on purpose, with a striped tomato gutter and a hatch overlay so you can't quote it out of context. The fix isn't to choose one mood; it's to give each its job. Chrome stays warm-neutral. Music stays chromatic. Type stays a duet. Edges stay crisp.
